01 / The opportunity

AI can understand what you mean. It rarely helps your body do it.

Many people face challenges with voluntary movement, fine motor control, tremor, rehabilitation, or learning precise physical actions. AURA-M explores an intelligent bridge from high-level intent to measured, supervised movement assistance.

THE GAP

Digital intelligence stops at the edge of the screen.

Today's assistants can interpret words, images, and tasks. They generally cannot guide a person's own movement, see how an attempt went, and adapt assistance in a measured loop.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Make AI a physical copilot — with the person still in control.

AURA-M combines perception, planning, safe action selection, physical assistance, and feedback. It aims to increase human capability and accessibility, not replace human judgment.

02 / One intention, a verified sequence

“AURA, pick up the cup.”

A flagship illustrative scenario shows how the same system architecture can translate a high-level goal into a sequence of symbolic actions, with a safety check and verification at every step.

INTENTION → ACTION

Plan a grasp the person can review.

  1. 01See the cup and estimate its location.
  2. 02Understand the requested task and user context.
  3. 03Plan reach, wrist orientation, hand open, grasp, and lift.
  4. 04Safety controller checks whether each validated action is allowed.
  5. 05Assist, measure the result, and verify before the next step.
HUMAN IN THE LOOP

The person remains at the center.

  1. 01The user chooses the goal and initiates the task.
  2. 02The user attempts the movement and remains part of the action.
  3. 03AURA-M observes and measures the attempt.
  4. 04Assistance is supervised, limited, and interruptible.
  5. 05Feedback supports progress toward independent performance.
03 / Closed-loop intelligence

Every physical action should be observed, checked, then verified.

AURA-M is not simply an AI command sent to a stimulator. It is a closed-loop architecture that returns to observation and checks the result before authorizing what comes next.

The system observes the person, target, and environment through camera and wearable sensing.
04 / Two distinct modes

Help with a movement — or help someone learn it.

Assistance and learning are related but different goals. AURA-M is designed to explore both without reducing learning to the system performing the task for the user.

MOVEMENT THE USER ALREADY KNOWS

Assist a known intention.

The user knows what they want to do but needs physical help performing it. AURA-M plans and assists a bounded movement while keeping the person involved.

USER GOAL→PLAN→SAFETY CHECK→ASSIST→VERIFY

Example: the user knows how to grasp a cup but has limited motor control. Assistance aims to support the user’s intended grasp, not to take over the task.

05 / AURA-M Skill Learning Mode

An AI physical instructor that helps a person become more independent.

The long-term vision is adaptive guided practice: understand a skill, teach a small part, observe the attempt, correct errors, and gradually reduce assistance.

LEARN → ATTEMPT → ASSIST → MEASURE → CORRECT → REPEAT

Teach the movement. Don’t just perform it for the learner.

For a fine-motor task such as drawing a shape, AURA-M could break the movement into hand position, wrist orientation, finger placement, trajectory, speed, and stopping. It would compare the attempt to the target and tailor feedback to the observed error.

ASSISTANCE → GUIDANCE → ADAPTATION → INDEPENDENCE
The goal is skill acquisition and growing user capability — not permanent dependence on the system.

07 / AI + deterministic safety

AI proposes symbolic actions. A separate safety layer decides what is allowed.

Raspberry Pi 5 (8 GB) is the proposed primary edge-computing platform. AI must never generate raw stimulation parameters or bypass the safety controller.

RASPBERRY PI 5 · PROPOSED

AI & edge compute

Proposed primary platform: Raspberry Pi 5 with 8 GB RAM. It can host perception, task understanding, motor planning, movement analysis, feedback, and logging components. Pretrained models are preferred over training a massive model from scratch.

AI ACTION → VALIDATED MOTOR PROFILE → SAFETY CONTROLLER → EMS/NMES. AI does not produce raw stimulation parameters.

08 / Multimodal perception

What is happening? How is it moving? What is the muscle doing?

Camera, inertial sensing, and optional muscle signals can complement one another. AURA-M should fuse signals rather than depend on a single source.

◉

Camera · “What is happening?”

Hand tracking, object detection, scene understanding, and visual movement verification.

⌁

IMU · “How is it moving?”

Arm and hand motion, orientation, speed, range, and movement characteristics.

∿

Optional EMG · “What is the muscle doing?”

Research sensing for muscle activation patterns, if suitable hardware and protocols are available.

✳

AI · “What should happen next?”

Interpret context and propose symbolic actions for deterministic validation.

11 / Piano is a benchmark, not the whole project

A measurable fine-motor test for a general platform.

Piano can expose whether a system sequences actions, times movement, coordinates fingers, verifies key presses, and helps a learner improve. AURA-M extends beyond music.

MIDI → VERIFIED ACTION

A closed-loop benchmark pipeline

  1. 01Read a piece or MIDI with music21.
  2. 02Map notes into a finger plan.
  3. 03Translate sequence into symbolic motor actions.
  4. 04Safety validate before supervised assistance.
  5. 05Detect keys and timing, verify, then correct.

12 / Safety is a hard boundary

If the system is uncertain or a fault is detected, the motor interface is off.

The dedicated deterministic safety controller sits between AI planning and physical assistance. Software cannot bypass the physical emergency stop.

1 · AISymbolic action only
2 · PLANNERMotor profile request
3 · SAFETY CONTROLLERWhitelist · limits · watchdog
4 · OUTPUTSupervised EMS/NMES
FAULT DETECTEDDefault-off state
OVERRIDEPhysical E-stop
FAIL-SAFE BY DESIGN · TARGET

AI cannot bypass the safety layer.

The AI proposes a symbolic action. The deterministic controller checks a whitelisted, calibrated profile and current system state. Only then can a supervised motor action be permitted.

AI failure · camera failure · tracking failure · communication loss · invalid state → MOTOR INTERFACE = OFF.

These are project design requirements. They are not proof that safety controls are implemented or verified.

17 / Responsible research

Assistive potential requires careful boundaries.

AURA-M is an engineering research platform concept. Any human-centered motor assistance work requires qualified supervision, appropriate approvals, and suitable equipment.

What AURA-M does not claim

It does not claim to cure paralysis, eliminate tremor, replace doctors or physiotherapists, or function as an approved medical device. It is not clinically validated.

What would be required for clinical application

Medical validation, ethics approval, appropriate human-subject protocols, regulatory compliance, clinically appropriate EMS/NMES equipment, and professional supervision.

Assistance must remain supervised

Stimulation profiles, electrode placement, and operation must be evaluated by qualified professionals. The system should default off on faults and preserve a physical emergency stop.

Concept imagery is illustrative

Generated or drawn wearable visuals on this page represent a design concept, not a completed prototype photograph or evidence of construction.
